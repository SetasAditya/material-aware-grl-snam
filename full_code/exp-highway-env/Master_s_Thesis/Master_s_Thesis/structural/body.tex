\begin{preface}
This thesis is about learning to move safely through unknown
environments: a problem that turns out to be about far more
than navigation.

The central idea, that a policy should be the flow map of a
structured energy landscape rather than an unstructured mapping
from observations to actions, emerged gradually over many failed
experiments and many conversations with my advisor.
The geometry-only foundation came first and worked better than
expected.
The material-aware extension came next and was harder than expected.
The synthesis chapter was the one I did not know I needed to write
until it was written.

Each chapter is an honest record of what was understood, what was
proven, and what remains open.
The open problems in Chapter~7 are not admissions of failure;
they are the most useful thing the thesis can leave behind.

I hope the framework developed here is useful to researchers who
want to build navigators that are not merely fast or accurate,
but genuinely safe, structured, and self-improving.
That remains the goal.

\hfill Aditya Sai Ellendula\\
\hfill Austin, Texas\\
\hfill May 2026
\end{preface}
